# TikTok, UAE and US

Written 13 September 2026. The worldwide motion from `GTM.md`, made into
videos you can record today on the TestFlight build, which already has the
new design, and post the day 1.0.1 is live.

---

## The one thing to get right

**Screen recordings beat AI-made ads for this app, and it is not close.**
TikTok's own data and every utility app that has tried both agree: a raw
recording of a phone doing the thing, with text on screen and a trending
sound, is watched and shared; a polished AI-generated advert is scrolled
past as an advert. Expyr's demo is fifteen seconds of a real screen doing
something a viewer can picture doing with their own phone. That is the
whole creative, and no AI can make it better than the app itself.

So: no AI video. Your phone, iOS screen recording, CapCut for the captions
and the sound. An hour for the first three.

**What AI is for here** is the copy: hooks, captions, hashtags, replies to
comments. All drafted below.

---

## Setup, once

1. A TikTok account named `expyr.app` or the closest free variant, business
   account, category Productivity. Bio: `Photograph it once. Never miss a
   renewal.` Link: the App Store URL.
2. Phone: Settings, Control Centre, add Screen Recording. Record in
   portrait. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification lands mid-take.
3. CapCut on the phone, free. Every video is: import the recording, trim to
   the beats below, add the on-screen text in the default bold white with a
   black stroke, add a sound from the trending list, export 1080 by 1920.
4. Seed the demo household in Expo Go if the recording needs a full list;
   use the TestFlight build for anything involving Pro or Expyr AI.

Post at 7 to 9 pm Gulf time for the UAE, which is also morning on the US
east coast, so one post reaches both.

## The three videos, recorded first

### 1. The subscription scan, worldwide hook

**Screen:** iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Screenshot. Open
Expyr, Add, Import my subscriptions, choose the screenshot. The list
appears with prices and dates. Scroll to the yearly total.

**Text on screen, in order:**
- 0s: `POV: you finally check what your subscriptions cost`
- 4s: `screenshot the list`
- 7s: `it reads every one`
- 12s: the yearly figure, big, alone: `$6,900 a year`
- 14s: `nothing touches your bank`

**Caption:** `It reads a screenshot. Nothing connected to your bank, nothing
reads your email. #subscriptions #savemoney #iphonetips #expyr`

**Length:** 15 seconds. This is the one that travels.

### 2. The passport scan, UAE hook

**Screen:** Timeline, empty or short. Tap the camera, photograph a passport
(yours, with the number covered by a finger, or the seeded one). The
category question if it appears, then the form with the date already
filled. Save. The Timeline row appears with the countdown.

**Text on screen:**
- 0s: `the AED 50 a day fine starts the day your grace period ends`
- 4s: `photograph the passport`
- 8s: `it reads the date`
- 11s: `reminds you weeks before`
- 14s: `and tells you what renewing costs`

**Caption:** `Visa, Emirates ID, Mulkiya, licence. Photograph it once.
#dubai #uae #expat #emiratesid #visa #expyr`

### 3. Ask the contract, both

**Screen:** Expyr AI, the Cherry Hills tenancy selected. Type "What does it
cost me if I move out early?" Send. The answer arrives with the clause
quoted under it.

**Text on screen:**
- 0s: `ask your lease a question`
- 5s: `it answers from the contract`
- 11s: `and quotes the clause`

**Caption:** `Read the contract once, ask it anything. #tenancy #renting
#lease #expyr`

## Then, one a day, from the same three ideas

Cut each of the three a second way: a different sound, a different first
line, the same screen. TikTok rewards volume and punishes polish. Ten
posts in the first two weeks, each fifteen seconds, each one idea.

First lines that work for utility apps, use any:
- `things I wish I knew before my visa expired`
- `the app I use so I never pay an overstay fine`
- `how much do you spend on subscriptions? (I was wrong by $2,000)`
- `iPhone feature nobody uses: screenshot your subscriptions`
- `tenancy contract hack for Dubai`

Reply to every comment in the first hour. A question in the comments
answered well is a second video's worth of reach.

## Paid, and the rule

**Organic first, two weeks.** The three videos tell you which hook works
before a dirham is spent, and TikTok's organic reach for a new account is
real in a way it is not on other platforms.

**Then a test, not a campaign.** TikTok Ads Manager, one ad group per
region, UAE and US, the best-performing organic video as the creative,
objective App Installs, **USD 10 a day each for seven days, USD 140 total.**
Attribution without an SDK: an App Store Connect campaign link per ad group
(App Analytics, Campaigns, Create), so installs show up in Apple's own
numbers by source, and nothing new is added to the app or the privacy
labels.

**Read it against `MONEY.md`.** An install is worth about USD 0.62 net at
the assumed conversion. Utility apps on TikTok typically pay USD 1 to 3 an
install, so expect the test to lose money per install and treat it as
buying a number: what an Expyr install costs in each region. If it comes in
under USD 0.40 anywhere, scale that region slowly. If it comes in over
USD 1, stop, and the answer is that paid waits for Pro conversion to be
measured at week eight, as planned.

**Never** run paid before 1.0.1 is live. Buying installs of the old scan is
buying refunds.

## What to count

Per video: views, shares, profile visits, link taps, in the account's
analytics. Per week: App Store Connect impressions and downloads against
the previous week. That is the whole dashboard.
