Expyr

Nothing expires unnoticed

Photograph a visa, an Emirates ID, a car registration or a tenancy contract. Expyr reads the expiry date, reminds you weeks ahead, and tells you what renewing actually involves: where to go, what it costs, and the fine for leaving it late.

What it does

  • Reads the date from a photo, a PDF or a screenshot
  • Reminds you weeks or months ahead, not the day before
  • Warns you when something else has to be renewed first
  • Points you at the right authority for your emirate
  • Keeps the whole family in one place

Where your documents stay

On your phone. The only thing that ever leaves is a photo you choose to scan, which is read once and then discarded. It is never stored and never used to train anything. The full details are here.

Built for the UAE

Renewal steps, costs and penalties have been checked against UAE sources, and vehicle and licence guidance follows your emirate rather than assuming Dubai. Elsewhere Expyr keeps your dates and reminders but stays quiet about the paperwork, rather than guessing.
