## Then the four recordings

Screen recording from Control Centre, portrait, Do Not Disturb on, Tools
button hidden if it is Expo Go, but use the TestFlight build. Each one starts
on the screen named and does the action slowly enough to follow.

1. **The list and the total.** Timeline, Subscriptions tab, at the top.
   Scroll slowly to the bottom, stop on the yearly total, hold two seconds.
2. **The import.** Timeline, tap the camera, Import my subscriptions, choose
   the iOS subscriptions screenshot, wait until the list appears with prices
   and dates, tap to save. The screenshot of your own iPhone's Subscriptions
   list is fine; nothing personal shows beyond app names.
3. **The passport.** Timeline, Documents tab, scroll once, tap Emirates ID
   or the US passport, let the page open on the countdown, scroll to How to
   renew and open it.
4. **The question.** Expyr AI, pick Villa tenancy, type "What does it cost
   me if I move out early?", send, wait for the answer with the clause.

Ten to fifteen seconds each. Put the four files in one folder and say where.
