# What Expyr is, in one line

Decided 3 November 2026, Hashem's call. Every store page, video, post and ad
works from this page. If something here is wrong, change it here first.

---

## The niche: subscriptions

**Expyr is a subscription tracker.** It still tracks documents, but they are
the second page, not the headline.

Why, in short: a general "tracks everything that expires" app grabs nobody.
Subscriptions win on every count that matters for getting noticed:

- **There is a number.** "Your subscriptions cost you AED 4,200 a year" is
  personal, slightly shocking, and shareable. A document reminder has no number
  like it.
- **It matters every month.** Charges come monthly, so the reminders keep
  coming and the app stays useful. A passport is renewed every 5 or 10 years,
  an Emirates ID or residence visa every 2 or 3.
- **People search for it.** "Subscription tracker" is one of the most searched
  phrases in the category. "Document expiry reminder" is not.

## The one promise

> See what your subscriptions cost you a year. Get warned before every charge.

## Why Expyr, and not the other trackers

The category is crowded, so "subscription tracker" alone is not a niche. These
three things together are:

1. **No bank login.** It reads one screenshot of your iPhone's subscriptions
   list. Nothing connects to your bank or reads your email.
2. **One payment, never a subscription.** The only honest way to sell an app
   about subscriptions.
3. **The yearly total.** What the year costs, before it arrives.

**Where this is strongest: the UAE.** The big bank-connected trackers such as
Rocket Money are, as far as we know, built for US banks. In the UAE, "no bank
login" is not a compromise; it is the only way that works.

So the audience is: **people who pay for several subscriptions, do not know
the total, and will not or cannot connect their bank.** UAE first.

## What we say, and what we stop saying

| Lead with | Mention second | Stop |
|---|---|---|
| The yearly number | "Your documents too": Emirates ID, visa, licence, lease | "Expiry reminders" as the headline |
| A reminder before every charge | Expyr AI, asking a contract a question | Passports or documents in the first screenshot, hook or line |
| No bank login, one screenshot | | "Everything that expires" |
| One payment, no subscription | | |

Document words (Emirates ID, visa, Mulkiya, Ejari) move into the store's
hidden keyword fields, so people searching for them still find the app.

No em dashes in any copy.

## Free and Pro

Decided 3 November, passed to App Development to build into the redesign:
**free shows you the problem, Pro fixes it.**

- **Free:** screenshot your subscriptions and see every one, with the real
  yearly total. Reminders for 2 of them. 3 documents.
- **Pro, one payment:** a reminder before every charge, unlimited documents,
  Expyr AI.

**Built 3 November** on the redesign branch, ships with the redesign:

- Every subscription a screenshot finds is added, on any plan, and every
  total counts all of them. Adding by hand is unlimited.
- Reminders go to 2 subscriptions: the ones the person picks, otherwise the
  two most expensive. The rest carry an amber "No reminder" tag, and iOS has
  nothing booked for them.
- After an import that leaves a charge without a reminder, the paywall opens
  with "You spend AED 4,200 a year on 7 subscriptions. Get a reminder before
  every charge."
- People already on free with more than 2 subscriptions keep reminders on all
  of the ones they had.

Until the redesign is live, the store still says "Track three documents and
two subscriptions free". That line changes when the redesign version's listing
is written.

## The gap to close

The screenshot import only finds subscriptions billed through Apple. Netflix,
Spotify or a gym paid by card have to be added by hand. If subscriptions are
the product, adding those has to take seconds, for example a list of popular
services with UAE prices to tap. Not decided yet; a question for App
Development and Hashem.

## How we will know it is working

- **Ads:** when they start, one week split between subscription searches and
  document searches, same budget, compare the cost per install.
- **TikTok:** per video, subscription hooks against document hooks, on views,
  profile visits and link taps.
- **The store:** product page views and first-time downloads, week on week,
  against the 30 days to 19 October: 89 views, 11 downloads.
