NO SIGN-IN IS NEEDED TO REVIEW THIS APP. Leave the credentials blank. Every
feature is reachable the moment the app opens.

WHAT IT IS
Expyr tracks anything with an expiry date and warns you before it lapses:
passports, visas, ID cards, licences, vehicle registration, insurance, tenancy
agreements, subscriptions and bills. Photograph it once; Expyr reads the date
off the picture, files it, and reminds you well before the day.

HOW TO SEE EVERYTHING
1. First launch: choose a country, an emirate if that country is the UAE, then
   allow or decline notifications. Both paths work.
2. Camera button: "Take a photo" or "Choose a photo" runs the scanner. "Enter
   it myself" adds an item by hand, no camera. Use the attached sample
   certificate, or any card with a printed date. Everything read is shown for
   confirmation first.
3. Open a saved item for the countdown, the reminder dates, renewal steps, cost
   and the link to the authority.
4. Expyr AI answers questions about a document it has read. A new install gets
   300 free credits so this can be tried without paying.

ACCOUNTS
Expyr works fully without one and most people never make one. It is offered in
one place for one reason: credit packs are consumables, and Apple keeps no
record of a consumable once used, so credits would otherwise die with the
phone.
Sign in: Settings, "Protect my credits" row, shown once the balance is above
zero. Sign in with Apple only; no password is created.
Delete: Settings, "Credits protected" row, Delete. It warns that unspent
credits are lost, asks Apple to confirm identity, then erases the identifier
and the balance immediately.
We request no scopes, so no name or email ever reaches us. Stored against an
account: an opaque Apple identifier and a credit balance. Nothing else.

IN-APP PURCHASES
One non-consumable, Expyr Pro (pro.lifetime), removes the free limits of 5
items and 10 scans. Settings, Unlock.
Three consumables, credits.small/medium/large, spent by Expyr AI: 10 credits
read a page, 20 answer a question. Settings, Top up.
Nothing renews and nothing is a subscription. All payment is through In-App
Purchase; no external payment, no web checkout, no third-party billing. No
physical goods or services are sold.

USE OF AI
Three features, all user-initiated, none running in the background.
1. Scanning: an image the user picks is sent once to our service and on to the
   Anthropic Claude API to read the expiry date and other fields. Values are
   shown for confirmation before saving.
2. Expyr AI: a document the user chooses is transcribed, then their questions
   are answered from that text.
3. Renewal guidance: steps and fees for a document type in a country are
   researched by a model using web search, cached and reused. Presented as
   indicative, with a link to the official source, never as official
   instruction.

WHAT LEAVES THE DEVICE
Only an image or document the user explicitly picks, at the moment they pick
it, plus questions they choose to ask. Images are never written to disk on our
server, are discarded once answered, and train nothing. Documents, dates,
notes and photos are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS, never
pushed. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no third-party tracking.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCE
Renewal steps, fees and fines are verified against primary sources for the UAE
only. Elsewhere the app hides them and says so during onboarding rather than
guessing. Everything else works identically in all 175 territories.

Expyr does not transact with any authority, collect any fee, or submit
applications, and is not affiliated with any government body.

PERMISSIONS
Camera: to photograph a document. Photo library: to open images the user
picks; it is never enumerated. Face ID: optional lock, off by default.
Notifications: local reminders, the core function.

iPhone only.
