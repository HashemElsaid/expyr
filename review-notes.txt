NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Every feature works on first launch without signing in. Leave Sign-In
Information blank; there is nothing to enter.

Sign in with Apple is optional and offered in one place: Settings, after
credits have been bought, so credits survive a change of phone. Credits are a
consumable and Apple keeps no record of a used one, so without it they would be
lost with the device. It stores an anonymous Apple subject identifier and a
credit count. We never receive a name or an email.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Settings, the Credits protected row, Delete. The user is warned that unspent
credits are lost and that documents stay on the phone either way, then Apple's
sheet confirms identity before anything is erased.

SEEING THE APP QUICKLY

1. Choose a country, then an emirate if it is the UAE. Allow or decline
   notifications; both paths work.
2. Tap + to add an item. "Take a photo" scans. "Enter it myself" needs no
   camera.
3. To test scanning, use the sample image attached here, or photograph any card
   with a printed date. Values are shown for confirmation before saving.
4. Open the item for the countdown, renewal steps, cost and late fine. The Ask
   tab answers questions about it. Settings has "Send a test reminder".

USE OF AI, STATED PLAINLY

Three features use a large language model. All user-initiated, none run in the
background, none required to use the app.

1. SCANNING. An image the user selects is sent once to our service, which
   forwards it to the Anthropic Claude API to extract the expiry date and other
   fields. Values are shown for confirmation before saving.
2. ASK. A saved document can be transcribed, then asked free-text questions.
   Answers are drawn from that document.
3. RENEWAL GUIDANCE. Steps, fees and processing times for a document type in a
   region are model-generated using web search, then cached and reused. It is
   presented as indicative, carries the wording "confirm with the official
   channel", and links to the official source. Never as official instruction.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

Expyr is free for 3 documents, 2 subscriptions and 10 scans. Expyr AI, which
reads a document in full and answers questions about it, is part of Expyr Pro
because it costs money every time it is used.

- ONE non-consumable: AED 149, paid once. Removes the three limits and
  includes 500 Expyr AI credits. Family Shareable, up to six people.
- THREE consumables: credit packs for Pro users who use Expyr AI heavily. Ten
  credits reads a page, twenty answers a question.
- NOT a subscription. Nothing auto-renews, nothing to cancel.
- No other way to pay: no external payment, no web checkout, no link out, no
  third-party billing, and nothing unlocked outside In-App Purchase. No
  physical goods or real-world services are sold.

AVAILABILITY

Expyr ships in all storefronts. Renewal guidance has been checked against the
responsible authority only for the UAE, and the app says so: elsewhere type
names go generic and guidance is labelled generated rather than verified.

Expyr collects no government fees, acts as no authority's agent, and is neither
affiliated with nor endorsed by any government body.

WHAT LEAVES THE DEVICE

Only an image or document the user explicitly picks, at the moment they pick it,
plus questions they ask. Nothing is written to disk on our server and no
question or answer text is logged by us. Both go to the Anthropic Claude API,
which may retain them briefly for trust and safety and does not train on them.
That is why the privacy labels declare them collected rather than claiming
none.

Nothing else is transmitted. Documents, dates, numbers, notes and photos are
stored only in the app's private storage on the device, including for signed-in
users: the service holds an identifier and a credit count and nothing else. Reminders are scheduled
locally by iOS, never pushed from a server. There is no analytics SDK, no
advertising SDK and no third-party tracking.

iPhone only.
