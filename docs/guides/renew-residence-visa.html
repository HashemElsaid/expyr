How to renew a UAE residence visa

A medical test, valid health insurance, and an application through your residency authority. Overstaying costs AED 50 a day.

Check this first

Fees and procedures change without notice. Before you act on anything below, confirm it at the ICP, which is the authority that actually handles this.

The short version

  • Where: GDRFA in Dubai, or the ICP app and website in the other emirates. Your employer or sponsor usually starts it.
  • Cost: AED 300 to 1,200 in government fees depending on visa type, with the medical and insurance on top.
  • Takes: 5 to 10 working days, including medical results.
  • If you are late: AED 50 a day once the grace period ends.
  • Valid for: 1 to 10 years depending on the visa.

The steps

  1. Establish who actually renews it. An employer, a sponsor, or you if you hold a Golden Visa or a freelance permit. This is the step people assume and get wrong.
  2. Take the medical fitness test at an approved centre.
  3. Renew or confirm your health insurance. The visa will not issue without it.
  4. Apply through your residency authority, or an accredited service centre.
  5. Your Emirates ID renewal is bundled into the same application.

The part people get wrong

Assuming the employer has it in hand. A lapse fines you rather than them, and the first you hear of it is often at the airport. If somebody else renews your visa, put a reminder on it anyway and ask them a month before, not a week.

The health insurance step also catches people out, because the insurance and the visa expire on different dates. Renewing the visa with lapsed cover stops the application.
