Thank you for the review. Answers to each point, in order.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached, captured on a physical iPhone running iOS 26, beginning at app
launch. It shows onboarding, adding a document by camera and by hand, the
countdown and renewal steps on a saved item, Expyr AI answering a question,
buying a credit pack through In-App Purchase, signing in with Apple, and
deleting that account from Settings.
Expyr has no user-generated content. Nothing a person enters is visible to
anyone else, nothing is published, and there is no feed, profile, comment or
message. There is therefore nothing to report or block.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Expyr tracks anything with an expiry date and warns you before it lapses:
passports, visas, ID cards, licences, vehicle registration, insurance, tenancy
agreements, subscriptions and bills. Missing one is expensive: a fine, lapsed
cover, a licence that stops you working, a subscription that renews for another
year. You photograph the document once; the app reads the date, files it, and
reminds you in good time with the steps for renewing. It is for anyone holding
paperwork that expires, particularly people living abroad, who hold more.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No account or credentials are needed; leave the sign-in fields blank. Every
feature is reachable on opening. Step-by-step instructions are in the Notes
field of App Review Information. To test scanning, use the sample certificate
attached to this submission, or any card with a printed date.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Anthropic (Claude API): reading the expiry date off a photographed document;
  answering questions about a document the user chose to have transcribed; and
  researching renewal steps for a document type. All user-initiated; none run
  in the background.
- Apple: Sign in with Apple for optional identity, and the App Store Server API,
  which our service calls to verify a purchase actually happened before any
  credits are granted.
- Google and DuckDuckGo favicon services: to fetch a subscription's own logo
  from its public domain. The request contains a domain name and no user data.
- Our own service, hosted on Render in Frankfurt, which holds credit balances
  and nothing else.
There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
One, deliberate and disclosed. Renewal steps, official fees, late fines and the
link to the responsible authority have only been verified against primary
sources for the United Arab Emirates. Outside the UAE the app does not show
them, and says so during onboarding rather than guessing. Everything else,
tracking dates, reminders, scanning, Expyr AI, backup and export, works
identically in all 175 territories.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND PROTECTED MATERIAL
Neither applies. Expyr is a personal reminder tool. It does not transact with
any authority, collect any official fee, or submit any application, and is not
affiliated with any government body. Official procedures are presented as
indicative, with a link to the authority's own site. Company logos shown
against subscriptions come from each company's own public domain.

7. IN-APP PURCHASES
One non-consumable, Expyr Pro (pro.lifetime), family shareable, which removes
the free limits of 5 tracked items and 10 photo scans. Reach it at Settings,
Unlock, or by adding a sixth item.
Three consumables, credits.small, credits.medium and credits.large. Credits are
spent by Expyr AI: 10 read a page, 20 answer a question. Reach them at
Settings, Top up. A new install is given 300 credits free.
Nothing renews and nothing is a subscription. All payment is through In-App
Purchase: no external payment, no web checkout, no third-party billing, and
nothing unlocked by any transaction outside it. No physical goods are sold.
