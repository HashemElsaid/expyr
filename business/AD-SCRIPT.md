# The Expyr ad, for an AI video tool

Written 13 September 2026. Two twenty-second ads built from the framed
screenshots in `Downloads\expyr-appstore-101` and the raw ones in
`Downloads\expy-new-screenshots`, with an AI voice. One for the UAE, one for
the US. Same structure, different first line and different last line,
because the two audiences are buying different things.

---

## The tools, and the order

1. **Voice first, ElevenLabs** (free tier is enough for two 20-second
   clips). Voice: a young male or female English voice with energy, not a
   narrator. Settings: stability around 40, style around 60, so it sounds
   like a person talking fast, not reading. Paste a script below, download
   the MP3.
2. **Video, CapCut on the phone or desktop.** New project, 9:16. Import the
   seven framed screenshots, the raw screenshots, and the voice MP3. Lay the
   voice on the timeline first, then place each screenshot on the beat the
   script marks, with the "Zoom in" or "Pan" animation on every still so
   nothing sits static for more than two seconds. Auto captions on, style
   bold white with black stroke, one line at a time. A low trending sound
   under the voice at 20 percent.
3. **If you want a tool to do the layout itself**, InVideo AI or Canva
   Video accept uploaded screenshots and a script and cut the edit. Paste
   the prompt at the bottom of this file. Check the result against the beat
   list; these tools invent text, and every word on screen must be one of
   ours.

Export 1080 by 1920, 30 fps. Under 25 seconds.

## The rules the tool must follow

- Every word on screen is from the caption column. No invented slogans.
- No stock footage, no people, no hands holding phones. The screenshots are
  the footage.
- The phone frames are the ones in `expyr-appstore-101`; do not let the tool
  draw its own device.
- Green is the brand colour, `#1D4B39`. Nothing else coloured except what
  is already in the screenshots.
- No music louder than the voice. No sound effects.
- The last frame holds the icon and "Expyr, free on the App Store" for a
  full second.

```
Make a 20-second vertical (9:16) TikTok ad for an iPhone app called Expyr
using only the uploaded screenshots as footage, with slow zoom and pan on
each one, no stock footage and no people. Use the attached voiceover MP3 as
the audio and cut to it exactly. Show these captions, one at a time, bold
white with a black outline, and no other text: [paste the caption column].
Brand colour #1D4B39. Hold the last screenshot with the caption "Expyr, free
on the App Store" for the final second. No music above the voice.
```
