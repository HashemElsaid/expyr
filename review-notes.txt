NO SIGN-IN, NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Expyr has no accounts, no login and no server-side user data. Every feature is
available on first launch. Please leave Sign-In Information blank.

SEEING THE APP IN TWO MINUTES

1. Choose a country on first launch, then an emirate if it is the UAE. Allow or
   decline notifications; both paths work.
2. Tap + to add an item. "Take a photo" runs the scanner. "Enter it myself"
   adds an item by hand with no camera at all.
3. To test scanning, use the sample image attached here, or photograph any card
   or letter with a printed date. Extracted values are always shown for
   confirmation before saving.
4. Open the item for the countdown, reminder dates, renewal steps, cost, late
   fine and the authority link. The Ask tab answers questions about it.
   Settings has "Send a test reminder", which fires a real notification.

USE OF AI, STATED PLAINLY

Three features use a large language model. All are user-initiated, none run in
the background, none are required to use the app.

1. SCANNING. An image the user explicitly selects is sent once to our service,
   which forwards it to the Anthropic Claude API to extract the expiry date and
   the other fields. Values are shown for confirmation before anything is saved.
2. ASK. The user may have a saved document transcribed, then ask free-text
   questions about it. Answers are drawn from that document.
3. RENEWAL GUIDANCE. Steps, fees and processing times for a document type in a
   region are model-generated using web search, then cached and reused. It is
   presented as indicative, carries the wording "confirm with the official
   channel", and links to the official source. It is never presented as
   official instruction.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

Expyr is free for 5 tracked items and 10 scans. Unlocking removes both limits.
Expyr AI, which reads a document in full and answers questions about it, is paid
for separately in credits, because it costs money every time it is used.

- ONE non-consumable: AED 149, paid once, removes the two limits. Marked Family
  Shareable, up to six people.
- THREE consumables: credit packs, spent as Expyr AI is used. Ten credits reads
  a page, twenty answers a question. A new install is given thirty pages' worth
  so it can be tried before it is bought.
- NOT a subscription. Nothing auto-renews, nothing to cancel.
- No other way to pay. No external payment, no web checkout, no link out, no
  third-party billing. Nothing is unlocked outside In-App Purchase.
- No physical goods or real-world services are sold.

AVAILABILITY

Expyr ships in all storefronts. Renewal guidance has been checked against the
responsible authority only for the UAE, and the app says so rather than
pretending otherwise. Elsewhere, type names fall back to generic ones, guidance
is labelled generated rather than verified, and features depending on checked
UAE data switch off. Everyone gets a working tracker; UAE users also get
verified guidance and the correct authority.

Expyr collects no government fees, acts as no authority's agent, and is neither
affiliated with nor endorsed by any government body. Links open official sites
in the browser.

WHAT LEAVES THE DEVICE

Only an image or document the user explicitly picks, at the moment they pick it,
plus questions they choose to ask. Nothing is written to disk on our own server
and no question or answer text is logged by us. Both are forwarded to the
Anthropic Claude API, which may retain them briefly for its own trust and safety
purposes, and does not train on them. That is why the privacy labels declare
Photos and Other User Content as collected rather than claiming none.

Nothing else is transmitted. Documents, dates, numbers, notes and photos are
stored only in the app's private storage on the device. Reminders are scheduled
locally by iOS, never pushed from a server. There is no analytics SDK, no
advertising SDK and no third-party tracking of any kind.

iPhone only. No iPad support is claimed.
