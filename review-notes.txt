NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Every feature works on first launch without signing in. Leave Sign-In
Information blank.

Sign in with Apple is optional, in one place: Settings, after credits are
bought, so credits survive a change of phone. It stores an anonymous Apple
subject identifier and a credit count. Never a name or an email.

IN-APP PURCHASES, ALL FOUR APPROVED

1.0.1 (7) was rejected under 2.1(b) for products not submitted. All four are
Approved in App Store Connect: pro.lifetime (Expyr Pro, non-consumable, Family
Shareable), credits.small, credits.medium, credits.large (consumable). The app
requests these four identifiers and no others. An Approved product offers no
Add for Review action and does not appear in the version's In-App Purchases
section, so there is nothing further to attach.

The last review ran on an iPad Air 11-inch, where this iPhone app runs in
compatibility mode. If a purchase could not be completed, please tell us the
screen and the message.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Settings, the Credits protected row, Delete. The user is warned that unspent
credits are lost and documents stay on the phone either way, then Apple's sheet
confirms identity before anything is erased.

SEEING THE APP QUICKLY

1. Choose a country, then an emirate if it is the UAE. Notifications optional.
2. Tap + to add an item. "Take a photo" scans, "Enter it myself" needs no camera.
3. To test scanning, use the attached sample image or any card with a printed
   date. Values are confirmed before saving.
4. Open the item for the countdown, renewal steps, cost and late fine. Settings
   has "Send a test reminder".

USE OF AI, STATED PLAINLY

Three features use a large language model. All user-initiated, none in the
background, none required to use the app.

1. SCANNING. An image the user selects goes once to our service, which forwards
   it to the Anthropic Claude API to extract the expiry date and other fields.
   Values are shown for confirmation before saving.
2. ASK. A saved document can be transcribed, then asked free-text questions,
   answered from that document.
3. RENEWAL GUIDANCE. Steps, fees and times for a document type in a region are
   model-generated with web search, then cached. Presented as indicative,
   carrying "confirm with the official channel" and a link to the official
   source. Never as official instruction.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

Free for 3 documents, 2 subscriptions and 10 scans. Expyr AI, which reads a
document and answers questions about it, is part of Pro because it costs money
every time it is used.

- ONE non-consumable: AED 149 once. Removes the three limits, includes 500
  Expyr AI credits. Family Shareable, up to six people.
- THREE consumables: credit packs for heavy Expyr AI use. Ten credits reads a
  page, twenty answers a question.
- NOT a subscription. Nothing auto-renews.
- No other way to pay: no external payment, no web checkout, no link out, no
  third-party billing. No physical goods or real-world services.

AVAILABILITY

Expyr ships in all storefronts. Renewal guidance is checked against the
responsible authority only for the UAE, and the app says so: elsewhere type
names go generic and guidance is labelled generated, not verified. Expyr
collects no government fees, acts as no authority's agent, and is neither
affiliated with nor endorsed by any government body.

WHAT LEAVES THE DEVICE

Only an image or document the user picks, at the moment they pick it, plus
questions they ask. Nothing is written to disk on our server and no question or
answer text is logged by us. Both go to the Anthropic Claude API, which may
retain them briefly for trust and safety and does not train on them. That is
why the privacy labels declare them collected rather than claiming none.

Nothing else is transmitted. Documents, dates, numbers, notes and photos stay
in the app's private storage: the service holds an identifier and a credit
count. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. No analytics SDK, no advertising
SDK, no third-party tracking.

iPhone only.
